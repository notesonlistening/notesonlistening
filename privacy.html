Privacy Policy

Last updated — June 2026

Who we are

Notes on LISTENING is an independent editorial publication about music psychology and the experience of listening. It is operated by Notes on LISTENING at notesonlistening@gmail.com.

What data we collect

We collect only one type of personal data: your email address, when you voluntarily subscribe to our newsletter via the form on this site.

We do not collect browsing data, cookies, or any other personal information. We do not use tracking pixels or third-party analytics on this site.

How we use your data

Your email address is used solely to send you the Notes on LISTENING newsletter — new issues and occasional updates related to the publication. We will never use it for anything else.

We do not sell, share, or rent your email address to any third party.

Newsletter service

Our newsletter is managed through Buttondown, a third-party email service. When you subscribe, your email address is stored on Buttondown's servers. You can read Buttondown's privacy policy at buttondown.email/privacy.

Your rights

You may unsubscribe from the newsletter at any time using the unsubscribe link included in every email. You may also request deletion of your data by contacting us at notesonlistening@gmail.com.

If you are located in the European Union, you have additional rights under the GDPR, including the right to access, rectify, or erase your personal data. To exercise these rights, contact us at the email above.

Changes to this policy

We may update this privacy policy from time to time. Any changes will be reflected on this page with an updated date. We encourage you to review it periodically.

Contact

Questions about this policy? Write to us at notesonlistening@gmail.com.
