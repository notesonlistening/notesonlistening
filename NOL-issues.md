## Editorial system — reminder

**Essay** — the "I" anchors, personal, emotional and lived side
**Deep Cut** — no "I", reader is inside, one idea pushed to the end. Same subject as Essay, opposite angle.
**The Year** — external gaze (journalists, entourage, witnesses) or artist. The angle nobody tells. The year follows the subject.
**The Question** — a question everyone can answer, specific memory
**Playlist** — personal, songs tied to the theme with one line each. The rest on Spotify.

**Essay + Deep Cut rule:** same subject, opposite angles. One feels, the other explains — or the reverse. Never both Your Brain on Music.

**Couleurs :** chaque rubrique a deux tokens distincts. `--cat-*` pour explore.html uniquement. Les tokens de page (`--terra`, `--or`, etc.) pour les issues. Voir README → Design system → Couleur par rubrique.

## Issue candidates — to develop

### After *(working title — also: Still)*
**Theme:** Posthumous. Albums and music released after an artist's death.
**Why a full issue:** Crosses three categories simultaneously — Never Met (parasocial grief), Off Record (the artist at their most private, sometimes darkest), The Pit (how a fandom processes this collectively). No other subject pulls all three this directly.
**Cases to consider:** Mac Miller / *Circles* (completed, family decision, received as a gift) — Juice WRLD (thousands of hours of sessions, posthumous releases still ongoing) — X, Lil Peep (each case structurally different).
**Core tensions:** Who decides what gets released. Whether unfinished work should stay unfinished. Whether we're being given a gift or watching grief be monetised. Whether the fan's need to have more is legitimate.
**Angle rule:** Treat with the rigor the project demands. Not a tribute. Not a verdict. The question open at the end.
